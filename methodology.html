Methodology

How this tracker works

A simple, transparent approach to comparing national polling averages.

Purpose

This site acts as an aggregator of aggregators. Instead of relying on one polling average, it compares several polling averages to get a broader view of the political environment.

Sources

The tracker compares national averages from sources such as RealClearPolitics, Silver Bulletin, The New York Times, CNN, Race to the White House, and Decision Desk HQ.

Average of averages

The current average gives each available source equal weight. Sources without an available average are listed, but they are not included in the calculation until a usable value is available.

Current calculations

The generic ballot average currently uses RealClearPolitics, Silver Bulletin, CNN, Race to the White House, and Decision Desk HQ. The New York Times is listed as unavailable for the generic ballot because no usable national generic ballot average is available.

The Trump approval average currently uses The New York Times, Silver Bulletin, CNN, RealClearPolitics, Race to the White House, and Decision Desk HQ.
